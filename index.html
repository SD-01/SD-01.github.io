About

Data work that has to hold up in production — and in the room where decisions get made.

I'm a Senior Data Engineer / Analyst with 5+ years at Capgemini (since November 2020), working across aerospace demand & supply, hospitality, and consumer billing portfolios. My work sits at the intersection of engineering and analysis: building the pipelines and data models underneath, and the dashboards and reporting on top that people actually use to decide.

That has meant ETL/ELT work in Python, SQL, Informatica, Spark/PySpark, Databricks, Snowflake, and Azure/AWS, paired with BI delivery in Power BI, Tableau, and IBM Cognos — plus the less visible work that makes it stick: data quality and governance, workflow automation, UAT, stakeholder engagement, and training the people who rely on the numbers.

I hold an MS in Analytics & Data Science (Business Analytics) from Georgia Tech (2025) and a BS in Industrial & Systems Engineering (Operations Research), Summa Cum Laude (2020). I'm based in Montreal and work fluently in English and French.
