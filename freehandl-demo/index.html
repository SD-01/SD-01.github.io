Questions, answered

What if the agent doesn't know the answer?

It says so honestly and hands the conversation to a real person on the FreeHandl team — with the full context, so your customer never has to repeat themselves. Nothing gets guessed, and nothing gets dropped.

Does it really work in French?

Yes — English and French are built in from the start, not bolted on. Your agent greets each caller in their language and can switch mid-conversation. Try the FR toggle in the demo above.

Is my business information private?

Your agent answers only from your business's own information — your services, prices, hours, and policies. That knowledge base belongs to your business, and your customer conversations aren't used to train anyone else's agent.

Do I need new software or a new phone number?

No. The agent connects to the phone number, calendar, and CRM you already use, and FreeHandl sets all of it up for you.

What does it cost beyond the package price?

Just the platform tools and usage, billed at cost — things like the voice/chat platform minutes your agent actually uses. The package price covers the build, the training, and the human backup.
